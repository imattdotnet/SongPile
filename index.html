STAGE PATCH & SIGNAL PATH

Standalone Analog Submixing (Notepad-5)

Why Analog XLR Beats Stage USB-C

FOH digital mixers act as USB devices, not USB hosts, and passive stage USB-C runs beyond 10–15 ft suffer clocking clicks and sync dropouts. The Soundcraft Notepad-5 runs standalone on stage power with zero latency.

Input / Bus Routing & Setting
Ch 1 (Combo) Guitar 1 via 1/4" TS · Hi-Z ON for bright pickup impedance
Ch 2/3 (Line) Guitar 2 active acoustic preamp (or passive electric via DI box)
Master L / R Balanced XLRs → Stage XLR wall jacks → FOH Line-level preamps
FOH Labels Guitar Submix L & Guitar Submix R (16-ch board)
